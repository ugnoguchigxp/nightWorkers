# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We aim to provide a respectful, harassment-free environment for all participants.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on technical facts and actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent and clarify disagreements with evidence.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after moderation warnings.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and discussions that violate this policy.

## Reporting
For conduct concerns, contact maintainers through the path in [SUPPORT.md](./SUPPORT.md).
